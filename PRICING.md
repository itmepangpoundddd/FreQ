## Who Should Choose What?

### 🆓 Personal — Free
> Perfect for hobbyists and learners

- You run FreQ at home for personal enjoyment
- You're a student learning about software development
- You want to try FreQ before committing to a paid plan

### 💼 Starter — $49/year
> For small businesses getting started

- Small store or cafe background music
- Personal radio station with < 100 listeners
- Single-location business
- Up to 5 devices

### 🏢 Business — $149/year
> For professional radio and multi-location businesses

- Online radio stations
- Hotel / restaurant / retail chains
- Multiple locations
- Need white-labeling or API access

### 🏛️ Enterprise — $499/year
> For large organizations with custom needs

- Broadcasting networks
- Government agencies
- Need source code access

## FAQ

### Can I try before I buy?
Yes! The **Personal** tier is free forever. Upgrade to commercial when you need it.

### What happens if I don't renew?
Your license reverts to Personal (free). Commercial features are disabled. Your data is preserved.

### Do you offer discounts?
- **Students**: 50% off any paid tier (with valid .edu email)
- **Non-profits**: 30% off any paid tier
- **Open-source contributors**: Free Business tier for active contributors

### How do I activate my license?
```bash
# CLI
freq license activate YOUR-KEY

# GUI
Settings → License → Enter Key → Activate
```

### Can I switch tiers?
Yes! Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect at next billing cycle.
